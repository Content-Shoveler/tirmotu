Immersive Comic Viewing Experience

Welcome to our Next.js-powered Progressive Web Application for viewing comics in a new, immersive way.

Features

  • Progressive Web App: Install it on your device for offline reading
  • Focus Points System: Guided viewing experience with smooth transitions
  • Smooth Animations: Polished transitions between pages and focus points
  • Dark/Light Mode: Automatic theme switching based on system preferences
  • Responsive Design: Optimized for all device sizes and orientations
  • Timeline Navigation: Easy navigation through pages and moments

How It Works

Our comic viewer transitions your view between specific "focus points" on each page, drawing your attention to key moments and creating a cinematic reading experience:

  1. Each comic page has pre-defined focus points that highlight specific regions
  2. The timeline at the bottom lets you navigate through moments
  3. Auto-play functionality guides you through the story at a measured pace
  4. Smooth zoom and pan animations create a dynamic reading experience
  5. Offline functionality lets you download comics for reading anywhere
